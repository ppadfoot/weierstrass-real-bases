## Arithmetic cases of Theorem 1.1

[`main_of_analytic_inputs`](Weierstrass/AlgebraicSplit.lean) splits by transcendence and by the absolute constant coefficient of the primitive polynomial. The transcendental case uses [`graph_and_measure_transcendental`](Weierstrass/GraphComparison.lean). For an algebraic base with enough predecessor choices, [`Branch.branching_anchored`](Weierstrass/BranchAnchor.lean) gives information one. For the other algebraic bases, [`PrimitiveEquation.contracting_root`](Weierstrass/AlgebraicSplit.lean) produces a contracting conjugate. [`graph_and_measure_contracting_conjugate`](Weierstrass/DescentTheorem.lean) gives the graph and measure conclusion for every admissible amplitude in this case.

## Further statements in the appendices

- The phase-dependent exceptional point in Appendix A has the cusp estimate and the local dimension claimed at the prescribed point. See [`cusp_drop` and `cusp_local_dimension`](Weierstrass/ExceptionalCusp.lean).
- At the beginning of Appendix B, [`Branch.phase_shift_ergodic`](Weierstrass/BranchErgodic.lean) proves that the shift by any nonzero integer is ergodic for Haar measure on the phase group. Its proof uses the dense physical orbit and contraction under inverse shifts.
- For Appendix B, the exact identity for the secant in a translated physical environment is [`Branch.increment_split`](Weierstrass/BranchRandomization.lean). [`Branch.auxiliary_haar`](Weierstrass/BranchRandomization.lean) identifies the law of that environment with the original Haar law. [`Branch.chain_cap`](Weierstrass/BranchChain.lean) and [`Branch.grid_retained_moment`](Weierstrass/BranchGridMoment.lean) apply the conditional estimates along the same digit stream at every scale. These declarations give [`Branch.anchored_of_slopeInformation`](Weierstrass/BranchAnchor.lean), which yields the subcritical graph lower bound through [`Branch.predecessor_graph_lower`](Weierstrass/BranchGraphLower.lean).
- For absolutely continuous measures, [`ac_sampled_dimension` in DensityCriterion](Weierstrass/DensityCriterion.lean) gives the local dimension criterion. The same file proves the integrated squared-density collision bound. [`Branch.goodPrefix_density_decay` and `Branch.grid_density_discard`](Weierstrass/BranchDensity.lean) apply this estimate to the genuine restarted digits and the finite grid under the manuscript’s integrable squared-density assumption.
