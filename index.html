Hausdorff dimension of Weierstrass cosine graphs for real bases

Timofei Iuzhakov

Manuscript for external review · Version 1.0.1 · 19 November 2026

Abstract

For real b > 1 and 1/b < λ < 1, the conjectured Hausdorff dimension of the graph of the classical Weierstrass function W(x) = ∑j≥0 λj cos(2πbjx) is 2 + log(λ)/log(b). We present a proposed proof for every real base. The argument also gives this dimension for the graph with any prescribed sequence of phase offsets and for its natural measure on each bounded interval of positive length. The lower bound is reduced to an estimate for small increments of a two-sided series. At transcendental bases the associated phases are independent under Haar measure. At algebraic bases the proof uses either the finite number of predecessors in the phase group or a contracting conjugate. In the latter case reciprocal changes of base and transverse differentiation propagate the increment estimate between amplitudes.
